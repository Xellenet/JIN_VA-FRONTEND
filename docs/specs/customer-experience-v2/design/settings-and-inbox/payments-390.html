Payment protection

How JinVa keeps your money safe until the work is done.

Where your money goes

Your payment waits with JinVa until you confirm the job is done.

  1. Step 1: You pay

    By card or mobile money, on Paystack’s secure checkout.

  2. Step 2: JinVa holds it

    The artisan can see the job is paid but can’t withdraw the money yet.

  3. Step 3: The artisan is paid

    Only after you confirm the job is done.

Refunded

If a dispute is decided in your favour, the money goes back to how you paid.

  • Card details are typed on Paystack’s page. They never reach JinVa, and JinVa never stores them.
  • Every payment is processed by Paystack. JinVa keeps a record of what you paid and which method you used.
