About

An operating system for the artisan services industry.

JinVa replaces fragmented, informal hiring with a structured platform for discovery, booking, payment and reputation-building.

What JinVa is

JinVa is a full-stack artisan services marketplace that connects clients with skilled, verified service providers across all trade categories — electrical, plumbing, carpentry, painting, cleaning, landscaping, beauty, and more.

It replaces fragmented, informal hiring with a structured platform for discovery, booking, payment, and reputation-building in one cohesive system.

The problem we're solving

Clients cannot easily evaluate an artisan's quality, price or availability. They rely on word of mouth, face unclear pricing, have no reliable booking or payment trail, and have no recourse when work goes wrong.

Artisans deal with inconsistent demand, frequent no-shows, no digital payment infrastructure, no way to showcase past work professionally, and no tools for schedule or reputation management.

Admins had no centralised view of platform health, could not enforce quality standards, and could not moderate disputes without fully manual processes.

What we're building toward

These are our objectives, not claims about where we are today:

  • To give clients transparent, trustworthy and frictionless access to verified artisans.
  • To give artisans tools to build a professional reputation, manage their schedule, and get paid reliably.
  • To give admins full visibility into platform activity, with tools to enforce quality and resolve disputes.
  • To establish a digital payment habit as the default transaction method across the platform.
  • To achieve measurable improvements in booking conversion, job completion and repeat client rates.

Who runs the platform

JinVa has an internal admin team that verifies artisans, moderates portfolios and reviews, oversees transactions and resolves disputes. Admin accounts are created internally and are not open for registration.
